Trivia

The Old Republic era in Legends spans thousands of years and includes multiple galaxy-scale wars between Jedi and Sith — far larger in scope than the movie-era conflicts.

Boba Fett survives the Sarlacc in Legends and later becomes Mandalore, leading the Mandalorians as a political and military figure.

The name “Coruscant” first appeared in the Heir to the Empire novel (1991) by Timothy Zahn, years before it showed up on screen in the prequel films.
