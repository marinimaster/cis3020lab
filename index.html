Overview

A discussion on how the movies relates to the old Star Wars Legends lore, mostly focusing on the books and other media that was released before the acquisition of Star Wars by Disney and rebooting the expanded universe content. This website's idea is to showcase the potential of these stories, and even debate wether Disney took the right decision to get rid of the already-written lore.

Content

  • Movies: A New Hope, The Empire Strikes Back, Return of the Jedi
  • Books: Vector Prime, Dark Tide I, Dark Tide II
  • Characters: Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, Leia Organa Solo, Mara Jade, The Yuuzhan Vong
