Community Q&A

How did you become a fan?

I became a fan after reading my first Legends novel and realizing how expansive and interconnected the universe truly was.

What do you love most about it?

I appreciate the depth of world-building and the moral complexity that many of the characters embody.

What would you recommend to someone just getting started?

I would recommend beginning with a well-known entry point like the Thrawn trilogy to understand the tone and scale of the Legends continuity.

Do you have any favorite fan communities or creators?

I tend to follow online discussion forums and long-form analysis creators who focus specifically on the Legends timeline and expanded lore.
